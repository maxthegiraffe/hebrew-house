ב · bet

Bet means house.

The second letter takes its name from bayit, a house. This one is built from old sounds, oud, darbuka, kanun, ney and a Hebrew choir, laid over house drums. Every set runs continuous, from Yemenite fire at 136 BPM to a lullaby at 78.

22 letters · 231 gates

Every letter is listening.

In Sefer Yetzirah the twenty two letters are set in a circle, each one joined to all the others: two hundred thirty one gates. Here every letter holds a slice of the sound, the kick on aleph, the cymbals up on tav. Watch them while it plays.

Where it started.

The first songs, made on Suno as @hebrewhouse. Almost all of them in A minor between 123 and 126 BPM, the pocket every set grew out of.
